Scholarship / Selected work

Research

My research investigates how AI systems can be developed and evaluated across linguistic, cultural, and social contexts. A central objective is to establish evaluation methods that provide valid, representative, and actionable evidence about model and system behavior.

01 / Research themes
01

Evaluation science and methodological validity

Developing robust evaluation frameworks and studying the strengths and limitations of automated evaluation, including LLM-based judges. This work considers measurement validity, disagreement between human and model evaluators, and the extent to which evaluation results generalize to actual use.

02

Multilingual and multicultural language technologies

Understanding model capabilities and disparities across languages and cultures, with work on code-switching, cross-lingual transfer, multilingual benchmarking, and data resources for languages that are underrepresented in mainstream evaluation.

03

Participatory and sociotechnical evaluation

Incorporating community perspectives and contextual evidence into AI assessment. The research combines computational evaluation with methods that examine the implications of AI in specific settings, including health and other consequential domains.

04

AI safety and system-level assessment

Studying multilingual and culturally situated safety risks, as well as evaluation of AI-enabled applications and agents under realistic conditions.
