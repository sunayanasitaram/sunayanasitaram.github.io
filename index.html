Biography

Sunayana Sitaram is an Assistant Professor at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, where she leads Plural AI Lab. Her research focuses on multilingual and multicultural AI, AI evaluation, and responsible AI, with emphasis on the Global South and other communities under-represented in AI.

Before joining IISc, she spent 11 years at Microsoft Research India, conducting fundamental and applied research and contributing to research leadership and community building. Her work spans multilingual natural language processing, code-switching, evaluation methodology, and the assessment of AI systems in real-world contexts. She received her PhD from Carnegie Mellon University in 2015 and previously directed the Microsoft Research India Research Fellow program.

Her current research examines the validity and representativeness of AI evaluations, the relationship between benchmark performance and deployment outcomes, and approaches that incorporate the perspectives of underrepresented communities.

Research interests

Selected research ↗
01

AI evaluation and measurement

Evaluation validity, multilingual assessment, LLM-based evaluators, and methods for assessing models and systems beyond conventional benchmarks.

02

Multilingual and multicultural AI

Methods, datasets, and empirical analyses addressing differences in model behavior across languages, cultural settings, and underrepresented populations.

03

Participatory and contextual evaluation

Human-centered and community-informed evaluation, with emphasis on how technical measurements relate to user experience and downstream outcomes.

03 / At IISc

Plural AI Lab

Plural AI Lab is an academic research group at IISc investigating multilingual and multicultural AI, AI evaluation, safety, and agentic systems, with an emphasis on the Global South.
