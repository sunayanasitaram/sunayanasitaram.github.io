02 / Invitations

Speaking invitations

When extending an invitation to give a talk, please include the proposed date, time, location, event format, expected audience, and topic. For events serving a limited or restricted audience, organizers are encouraged to consider appropriate speaker compensation.

01

Outside Bengaluru

Please provide at least one month's notice for invitations involving travel outside Bengaluru.

02

Within Bengaluru

Please provide at least two weeks' notice for local speaking engagements.

The notice periods are intended to allow adequate planning and do not guarantee availability.
