Plural AI Lab

Plural AI Lab investigates the development and evaluation of AI systems across diverse languages, cultures, and contexts. Our research brings together foundational methods in Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing and Human-centered AI with questions of reliability, representation, and societal relevance.

01 / Research programme

Research directions

01

Multilingual and multicultural AI

Language technologies and evaluation methodologies that account for linguistic diversity, cultural context, and variation in user needs.

02

AI evaluation and safety

Reliable assessment of capabilities, limitations, and potential harms, including research into evaluation metrics, models used as judges, and contextual safety assessment.

03

Deployed AI solutions

Evaluation of AI agents and applications in realistic workflows, with particular attention to the relationship between technical performance and practical outcomes.
